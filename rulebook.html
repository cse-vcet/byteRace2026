01 About the event

Byte Race 2026 is a 6-hour project-development hackathon held in three stages: Round 1A, the Idea Presentation/project submission; Round 1B, the Knowledge Grid; and Round 2, the Final Race. Students of any branch and any year are welcome to take part, and the top 10 teams go on to the Final Race.

03 Eligibility

  • Open to all branches and all years of VCET.
  • Each team must have 2 to 4 members.
  • All members of a team must be from the same year and branch.

04 Registration

  • Registration opens on 1 October and closes on 4 October 2026.
  • Registration requires the team name, each member's details (name, USN, branch, year, phone number and email) and the team leader's contact.
  • The Idea Presentation/project submission is part of registration. A team that does not submit is not eligible for the quiz.
  • Team members cannot be changed after the deadline without the organisers' approval.

05 Round 1A & 1B: Filtration rounds

Round 1A is the Idea Presentation/project submission, followed by Round 1B, the Knowledge Grid. Together, they decide which teams reach the Final Race.

5.1 Round 1A: Idea Presentation

  • Each team comes up with a project idea and submits an Idea Presentation as a PPT.
  • The project is judged on originality, uniqueness, usefulness and presentation.
  • Teams that include a live demo receive bonus points.
    • Teams may submit a public GitHub repository link with a clear README covering the problem, the solution, the tech stack, setup steps, team details and a live demo link, if available.
  • Submissions made after the deadline will not be considered.

5.2 Round 1B: Knowledge Grid (offline quiz)

  • A 50-mark offline quiz, conducted on Kahoot or a similar platform.
  • The venue will be announced later.
  • The quiz is held after project submissions close.

5.3 Shortlisting

  • The initial screening is based on the Idea Presentation/project submission and Knowledge Grid scores.
  • The top 10 teams are selected for the hackathon.
  • The organisers' decision is final.

06 Round 2: The Final Race

Date
28 Oct 2026
Start time
09:00 AM IST
Duration
6 hours non-stop
  • The problem statements are announced at the start of the round. Each team picks any one of them.
  • Teams build a working prototype or MVP within the 6 hours.
  • Work must start at the announced start time. No pre-written project code is allowed. Existing libraries, frameworks, templates and AI tools may be used.
  • Teams must use a fresh GitHub repository, created at the start, with commits (preferably atomic commits) throughout.
  • AI tools are allowed. Every team member must be able to explain the project.
  • Teams must bring their own laptops.
  • The final code must be pushed within the 6-hour Final Race. Anything pushed later will not be judged.

09 Code of conduct & rules

  1. Respect participants, organisers, judges and college property.
  2. Projects must not contain abusive, discriminatory or inappropriate content.
  3. Teams must not interfere with other teams' work, network or systems.
  4. The judges' decisions are final and binding.
  5. The organisers may change dates, venue or rules if needed, and will inform participants.
  6. Participants must carry their college ID cards at all times.
