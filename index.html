Oct 1–4

Registration & project submission

Teams register and submit their 48-hour project as a GitHub link, with a live demo if applicable. Any AI tool is fair game.

50-mark quiz

RESULTS

Round 1 results announced

The 10 shortlisted teams are revealed a day after the quiz wraps.

Shortlisted teams build live against a set of 5–6 domains. Winners are judged on performance across both rounds, not just the final demo.
